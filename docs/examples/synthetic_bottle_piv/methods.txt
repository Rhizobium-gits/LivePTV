Velocity fields were obtained by particle image velocimetry (LivePTV) from 150 frames recorded at 30.00 fps (5.0 s of camera time; velocities use camera timestamps). Each frame first had a temporal background removed (running mean with a memory of 300 frames), which suppresses static reflections, labels and scratches on the container. Pixels belonging to static glare (background ≥ max(200, median + 60) grey levels), moving saturated glints (≥ 250 grey levels), everything outside the fluid and a 1.5 mm band inside the container wall were masked (dilated by 3 px); interrogation windows more than 40 % masked were not evaluated. Images were normalised by local contrast (box radius 12 px). Cross-correlation was computed by FFT in 64→32 px interrogation windows (multi-pass with symmetric sub-pixel window offset, 8-tap Lanczos (windowed-sinc) interpolation of the shifted windows, 2 final iterations, 50 % overlap), with a 3-point Gaussian peak fit corrected for the window-overlap bias (Westerweel 1997). Vectors with a normalised correlation below 0.20 or failing the normalised median test (threshold 2.0, ε = 0.10 px; Westerweel & Scarano 2005) were rejected and replaced by the median of their neighbours (excluded from statistics). 100.0 % of the evaluated vectors were valid (34.1 % of windows masked). The random uncertainty of single vectors, estimated from correlation statistics (Wieneke 2015), had a median of 0.016 px per pair (95th percentile 0.049 px). The particle-image diameter inferred from the correlation-peak width was 3.7 px (peak-locking index 0.00). Refraction by the container was corrected by tracing the camera ray of every image point through the wall into the measurement plane (Snell's law in 3-D; cylinder ⌀60.0 mm, wall 2.00 mm (n=1.474), fluid n=1.333, plane +0.0 mm from the axis, distant camera; refraction corrected; lateral magnification at the centre 0.750). Displacements were mapped end to end, and the time-averaged field was resampled on a regular grid in true plane coordinates. The length scale was 0.12 mm per pixel (container silhouette (⌀60.00 mm outer)). Statistics were accumulated on a 26 × 25 grid (16 px cells); 630 cells had at least 10 samples. The statistical uncertainty of the mean is the standard error of the samples in each cell (median 0.2 % of the local speed).
